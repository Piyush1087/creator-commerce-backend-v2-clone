# BS-01 — General Settings authority reconciliation

Classification: `FUNCTIONAL_STATE_RECONCILIATION`.
Base: `program/brand-settings-mvp` at
`76421a49d4e37eb77780d2fac4243ee9b9805c81`, verified after fetching origin.
Branch: `settings-mvp/bs-01-general`. No migration or schema model changes.

General reads the operational workspace/organization name from the authorized
Brand's related `Organization.name`. Organization-name mutation updates that
row only; it
does not mutate protected Brand name, country, currency, domain, or industry.
No Organization is created implicitly when the relationship is unavailable.

The strict General mutation schema accepts only `firstName`, `lastName`, and
the compatibility wire key `organizationLegalName`, which represents the
operational Organization name rather than registered legal billing identity.
Unknown fields, including legacy geography, currency,
address, tax and email fields, reject the entire HTTP request with 400.
The existing User.name split/partial update behavior remains unchanged. Every
active member may update their own personal name. Owner and Finance Admin may
also update Organization.name; Campaign Manager organization and mixed requests
are denied atomically. Explicit active membership is still required; no
membership admission or reactivation was added.

GET retains null address/tax compatibility keys; Billing remains their authority.
The legacy personal avatar key is now null, while the Brand logo remains in
`brand_identity.logo_url`. Account email and Brand identity are read-only.
The complete Team payload mapping and all Team service/controller behavior are
unchanged. Instagram/OAuth and BS-07 authorization implementation are unchanged.

Two mechanical Co-pilot adaptations are necessary: omit absent legacy fields
from its General confirmation input while retaining/rejecting explicit legacy
requests, and render a missing organization name with its existing placeholder.
No other Co-pilot actions or financial behavior change.
